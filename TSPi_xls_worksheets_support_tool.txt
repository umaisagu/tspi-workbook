
TSPi.xls worksheets


Worksheet forms support

1. TSPi.xls includes these TSPi forms implemented as worksheets.

	a. Defect Recording Log (LOGD)
	b. Time Recording Log (LOGT)
	c. Schedule Plan Template (SCHEDULE)
	d. Program Plan Summary (SUMP)
	e. Quality Plan Summary (SUMQ)
	f. Size Summary (SUMS)
	g. Task Plan Template (TASK)

2. Other worksheets included with TSPi.xls, and their purposes, are as follows.

	a. Instructions: step-by-step instructions for planning and tracking
	b. Project: a project summary
	c. Team: team member information
	d. Roles: role assignments
	e. Defect Types: the defect types allowed on the LOGD worksheet
	f. QprofParam: the parameters used for calculating the five points on a quality profile chart
